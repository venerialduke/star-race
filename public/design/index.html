Finding the game

Each round, four frameworks are proposed, three reviewers score them, and one recommendation is written for the owner to answer. Newest first.

  • Round 5 · Fast Ships Swing WideThe faster a ship goes into a bend, the wider and less predictably it swings off the golden path; the player builds the ship, plans the route and crew before the race, and taps at most three times, inside timing windows the crew's reaction sets.2026-09-11
  • Round 4 · Fast Ships Answer LateThe faster a ship goes the later your taps land, so every decision is about when to leave the golden path where the speed and the energy are.2026-09-09
  • Round 3 · The Ledger and the CardThree ships, one course, and every credit outside the purse sitting in a shared pool: all three pilots declare a route blind, the declarations are revealed before anyone commits, and then one bar — charge — is at once your speed, your shields, your ammunition and your income.2026-09-08
  • Round 2 · Raise or ClearBuild from The Toll's spine — every ship makes one move on the course every heat — but swap its eight tag rules for four declared Drives, replace the unforecastable toll with a rising ante and a real purse spread, and let the ladder become a head start in a single final.2026-09-07
  • Round 1 · The Cut, Two CardsBuild from The Cut: an eight-ship, seven-heat season on one points ladder with a cut after every heat from the third, plus two named hazard cards per course (leader lays first, tail lays second with the leader's visible), tags with pips and a capped modifier block, tier-gated face-up drafting in reverse ladder order, and hull that carries and costs credits to repair.2026-09-06
