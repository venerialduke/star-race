The track

checkpoint → sector → checkpointa sector may offer several splits — the paths through it — and every split ends at the same next checkpoint · some checkpoints are pit stops
Golden paththe bright middle: faster, charges abilities
Wideslower · hazards that hurt — power into one and it may shove you to another split · pockets that pay
Fixturesobjects you own on a sector, shown to your heat · or dropped mid-race by an ability, seen by nobody
Black holesterrain as well as hazard: a corner can be caused by one · the right engine reads it as an opening and takes no damage
Augmentsa split or a whole sector you add — one heat, or all season
Growthsame start for all · grows between phases · more pit stops as it grows so a run stays ≤30 s

How ships reach each other

Still no contactships never touch · everything below reaches a rival through the track, the economy or the shop
Push them off coursegravity mines · tractor beam (ships and fixtures) · a boost's black hole · the swing is no longer the only way off the path
Hurt themmissiles, short to long range · a deflected hazard
Take their shota full shield can keep the weapon that hit it — their fire, your salvage
Shape what they flyfixtures and augments you own sit in your heat's track

Money & points

Purseby finish order · the biggest sum · winning must pay best
Poolsbounty by damage · solar (a pool still? open) — shared, thin as more draw, capped under the purse
Dark matterinventory, not a pool: collected in the race, feeds a boost, cashed in with a L3 collector
Salvageweapons and fixtures your shields caught, sold when the race ends
Interest & billsheld credits earn, to a cap · hurt crew costs · the ship has a maintenance bill
Spendingcomponents · their upgrades · more slots · fixtures and augments · repairs
Pointsfinish order + margin — a close loss is worth about twice a beaten one · never spent · the cut line reads them
Declarationsbet before the heat: Long Line wide at every split · Hold the Line never leave the path · win big, lose points
