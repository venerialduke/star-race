# Notes on Proposal (Set 2)

## "Whole idea in one paragraph" section
- The whole game is not just that one tension. Not sure the "whole idea in one paragraph" framing is the best thing.
- Might be better to have a summary of the mechanics, in a few sentences:
  - What is the stage design?
  - What does a ship do (stats) / how does a build come together?
  - How does the track evolve?

## Speed / Thrust
- It's not clear how speed and thrust are determined.
- The player IS NOT controlling the ship during the race — it's auto-battle at its core.
- A build with a certain thrust value + crew + navigation (plus some pre-race decisions) should somehow determine speed/acceleration. We need to think about that and then describe it.
- Idea: based on ship stats/build, there's a pre-game "how to approach corners" selection focused on things like braking and acceleration in the turn.
- Could be neat if a crazy thrust build let a player overshoot INTO another split of the track.
- Better navigation systems could help auto-choose based on goals (optimize for lap time, optimize for resource collection, etc.).

## Taps During a Race
- Not completely sold on taps.
- Alternative: the ship has a set of abilities (based on the build) that charge up over the race and get used when "optimal."
  - Downside: makes things like targeting other ships or choosing where to set mines tough.
- Don't limit this to a set number yet — maybe just list a few as examples. Needs more thought.
- Could be that placement of fixtures just happens before the race starts.

## Crew Maintenance
- If crew members die, get injured, or need repair, there are maintenance costs between rounds (maybe).

## Influencing the Track
- Are fixtures the only way a player can influence the track?
- Some builds might allow the player to add a split of a certain type, or even add a track section (temporarily or permanently).

## Wanted / Cop Mechanic
- Not sure we need it right now. Remove it to keep things simple.

## Eliminations
- Rather than eliminating the bottom two, use a set cutoff — so technically there could be more or fewer eliminations than "normal" throughout the season.
- We say lowest on "points," but it's not clear where "points" live and how they differ from "credits."
- I do like heat "declarations." Could think about another example to go along with "Long Line."

## Between-Race Economy
- Between races we make choices about what to do with our money.
- Autochess games usually have an "interest" concept (more $ the more you save, up to a point). We can have that too, but could also think about other ways to "hold and play for the long term."
- We should have between-lap purchase options or something — we want more frequent purchase decisions/moments.
- Idea: break points (pit stops) during each lap with a short window to make changes/additions; then each lap there's a more comprehensive break, opponents switch, next lap, etc.
  - That gives 1 heat == 1 + 3 pit stops + 3 purchase opportunities per heat.
- Interest might only collect between laps, or even between heats — we'll have to play around with it.

## Scope of Open Questions
- There are several "needs input for decision" items, but they're all too in the weeds.
- We're trying to hone the overall general mechanics and systems first; design + balance comes later.
