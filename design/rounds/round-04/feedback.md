# Notes on Proposal

## Clarity / Jargon
- The single paragraph has jargon that isn't defined or clear, e.g. "order you tap lands late" — we don't know what "lands" or "tapping" mean here.
- Not sure what a "Rake Hit" is.
- It's not clear what causes eliminations.

## Numbers vs. Mechanics
- Too much focus on actual numbers (stat points, etc.).
- We want to focus on mechanics, not values. Example: "shields regenerate slowly" is good because it describes a mechanic — the actual point values don't matter.

## Handling
- I don't like the "reaction lag" interpretation of what handling and reaction time imply. It's a fixed formula, so players would just tap early.
- Alternative: when the track/path a ship takes has a curve, there's a swing-out or overshoot, with some randomness, based on the handling stat and how handling interacts with speed.

## Reaction Time
- Less about the player's actual reaction time; more about how the crew deals with known or unknown events.
  - Enemy fires a missile at us — does the crew automatically shift shields into the right spot?
  - How well / how much can they collect when passing through a resource collection zone?
- Possible extension: a "tap at the right time" mechanic, where the "right time" window is easier (wider) with higher reaction time.
- Working definition: reaction time is a stat derived from current speed/acceleration plus crew type / crew stats.

## Tapping During a Race
- Some small amount of tapping during the race is OK — 0–3 times max (or 0–3 is optimal).
- Need to think more about *what* tapping does during a race and *why*.

## Route Planning
- We plan a route before the race. When sections/subsections have splits/options, we plan the route out — at least for the parts we can see.
- Some parts of the track may not be fully known before starting, so there may be:
  - Blind picks at a track split, without seeing what the zone contains.
  - No ability to pick at all — direction is random, or weighted random based on crew / reaction time.
- Navigation systems help reveal these things better during planning.

## Laps / Stages
- I still like multiple laps per stage: the track is the same but "growing" each lap, and the variation in a given heat is just who you're facing and what your choices are.
