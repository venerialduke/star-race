# Season structure

The shape of a season from the first choice to the last cut. Parameters,
confirmed rather than invented: what round 7 said, plus what the notes added
or changed.

## Stages

| id         | stage      | what happens                                                                                                                                                                                         | from    | status |
| ---------- | ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- | ------ |
| opening | Opening shop | No frame. The player starts with an **initial budget** and a stocked shop, and deploys what they choose into four slots. | build call, 2026-09-13 | seeded |
| pacing-lap | Pacing lap | Two laps of the initial track with one pit stop, no opponents. Paid by your time against the track's benchmarks, not against anyone. The pit stop and the end of lap one are purchase opportunities. | notes   | seeded |
| heat       | Heat       | Two laps against the same three ships, a pit stop at the end of each lap. Scores once, by total time and margin.                                                                                     | round 7 | seeded |
| phase      | Phase      | Several heats. Groups redrawn between heats. The track grows at the end.                                                                                                                             | round 7 | seeded |
| cut        | The cut    | At the end of a phase, everyone below a set points line is out, however many that is.                                                                                                                | round 7 | seeded |

## Notes

**2026-09-13, the build call.** Frames are cut. The season opens with an
initial budget and a stocked shop rather than a named starting kit; element 1
becomes "Starting shop" and holds what that stock and budget are. The pacing
lap is unchanged: round 7's loop began at the shop before the first heat. The pacing lap is the first time a
player sees the track and the first credits they earn. Whether the cut could
also fall between heats was raised in round 6's notes and left open in round
7; nothing here decides it.
