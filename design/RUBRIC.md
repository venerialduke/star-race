# How a framework proposal is scored

Reviewers score every proposal on each criterion from 1 to 5, and write one
line of justification per score. A 3 is "adequate and unremarkable"; a 5 is
"this is the reason to pick this proposal"; a 1 is "this sinks it". Totals are
out of 40.

Scores are a tool for comparison, not a verdict. A reviewer's written case
matters more than the sum, and a proposal with one 5 and one 1 may be worth
more than a proposal of 3s, because the 5 can be grafted onto something else.

| #   | Criterion                  | The question                                                                                                                                                              |
| --- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Build fantasy**          | Does assembling a build feel like a plan coming together? Are there identifiable archetypes, synergies that reward commitment, and a moment where a build "comes online"? |
| 2   | **Tradeoffs**              | Do the economy and health systems force real short-run versus long-run choices every round? Is saving ever right? Is greed ever right? Is either ever obviously right?    |
| 3   | **Interaction**            | Do other players' builds and choices change my race and my build? Is there counter-play, information to read, and something to deny or contest?                           |
| 4   | **Stakes and recovery**    | Can a player be knocked out, is it earned rather than sudden, and can a bad early round be recovered from?                                                                |
| 5   | **Legibility**             | Can a player on a phone understand what happened in a round and why, in the time the round takes? Are the systems few enough to hold in one head?                         |
| 6   | **Fit with the two rules** | Pure sim, fixed tick, ships never touch, one thumb. Does it fit, and where it bends a rule does it say so and earn it?                                                    |
| 7   | **Buildable**              | Is the cost honest? How much of the current sim survives? Could the first playable version of this be built in a handful of one-session PRs?                              |
| 8   | **Measurable**             | Could a harness of bot strategies tell us whether choosing well pays, whether any archetype dominates, and whether the run is winnable?                                   |

## What reviewers also deliver

Beyond scores, each reviewer names:

- **The best single idea** in each proposal, whether or not the proposal as a
  whole wins.
- **The fatal flaw**, if there is one, in each proposal.
- **A recommended merge**: which proposal to build from, and what to graft onto
  it from the others.
