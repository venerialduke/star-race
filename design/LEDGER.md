# Ideas ledger

Every idea a round considered, and what became of it. Append-only: a round adds
its own section and never edits an earlier one. If a later round changes its
mind about an idea, it adds a new row saying so and citing the round it is
overturning.

Status is one of: **adopted** (in the current recommendation), **rejected**
(with the reason), **parked** (good, not now), **open** (waiting on the owner).
